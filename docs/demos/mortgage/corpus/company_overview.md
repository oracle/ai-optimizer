# Gonzap Home Finance — Company Overview

Document status: Fictional demonstration material. Gonzap Home Finance is not a real lender, and this document is not lending advice.

## Purpose

Gonzap Home Finance is a fictional residential mortgage lender used to demonstrate how application data and policy documents can support a mortgage-review conversation. The demonstration covers purchase and remortgage applications for residential properties.

## Illustrative lending approach

The demonstration considers capital-and-interest mortgages across a range of terms and product rates. Its review process brings together verified income, committed expenditure, household expenditure, dependants, property value, requested loan amount, loan-to-value (LTV), and loan-to-income (LTI).

Affordability is considered separately from the property value or deposit. The review also considers a stressed monthly mortgage payment and any material changes that could affect a customer's ability to repay.

## Income and evidence

Gonzap Home Finance may consider permanent employment, fixed-term contract income, self-employed income, and other evidenced income. Income must be independently evidenced and assessed for sustainability. Where income is variable or continuity is relevant, the review may require additional evidence.

## Review outcomes

The corpus supports illustrative policy screening only. It can identify whether an application is within an illustrative threshold, requires enhanced review, needs evidence, or is outside an illustrative threshold. It does not approve or decline an application; a human reviewer must consider the complete application, supporting evidence, and applicable obligations.
