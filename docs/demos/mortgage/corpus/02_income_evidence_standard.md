# Harbourstone Home Finance — Income Evidence Standard

Document status: Fictional demonstration policy. Not a real lender policy or lending advice.

## Evidence expectations

Use evidence proportionate to the income being relied on. The demonstration application packs state only that evidence is present or requires review; they do not include documents, names, account numbers, payroll references, or other personal data.

- Permanent employed: verify basic salary using independent evidence covering a recent period.
- Fixed-term contract: verify current contract end date, role continuity, and evidence of prior contract history where income continuity is relevant.
- Self-employed: review independently prepared income evidence over a representative period and investigate material year-to-year movements.
- Other income: include only income that is independently evidenced and considered sustainable.

## Expenditure and resilience

Capture monthly committed credit payments, household expenditure, dependants, and known future changes. The illustrative stress rate is 7.00%. A high-LTV case, a contract-income case, or a case with elevated committed expenditure may require enhanced review even if it is within an income multiple.
