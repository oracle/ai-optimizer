# Harbourstone Home Finance — Residential lending criteria

Document status: Fictional demonstration policy. Not a real lender policy or lending advice.

## Purpose and scope

This policy describes the illustrative first-line screen for capital-and-interest residential purchase and remortgage applications. It supports a review conversation; it does not produce an approval or decline.

## Core screening measures

- The illustrative maximum loan-to-value (LTV) is 90%. Applications above 85% LTV require a documented review of affordability resilience and deposit source.
- The illustrative maximum loan-to-income (LTI) is 4.50 times total verified annual income. This is a portfolio-screening cap, not a substitute for an affordability assessment.
- The affordability assessment considers verified income, committed expenditure, essential household expenditure, dependants, and a stressed monthly mortgage payment at a 7.00% annual rate.
- A reviewer must confirm that any material income variation, expected expense change, or property-specific cost is understood before an outcome is recorded.

## Employment and income

Permanent employment may use evidenced basic salary. Variable income may be considered only when evidence demonstrates a sustainable pattern. Fixed-term contract income requires evidence of the current contract and continuity of employment. Self-employed income requires the evidence described in the Income Evidence Standard.

## Important limitation

The property value, deposit, LTV, or expected house-price movement must not be used as evidence that repayments are affordable. A complete assessment needs evidence and human review.
