# Gonzap Home Finance — Affordability Review Playbook

Document status: Fictional demonstration guidance. Not a real lender policy or lending advice.

## Illustrative review flags

Escalate the application for enhanced review when one or more of the following applies:

- LTV is greater than 85%;
- LTI is greater than 4.00, especially where contract or variable income is used;
- committed expenditure is high relative to verified income;
- there are dependants or a foreseeable household-cost change that could reduce resilience;
- the evidence needed to support income continuity is incomplete.

## Review outcome language

Use neutral, non-decisive language: `within the illustrative threshold`, `requires enhanced review`, `evidence required`, or `outside the illustrative threshold`. Do not say an application is approved or declined. A human reviewer must consider the complete application, evidence, and applicable obligations.
